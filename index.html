docx, xlsx and pptx — right in your browser

Open Word files (.docx), Excel files (.xlsx) and PowerPoint files (.pptx) as they look in Microsoft Office.

ibisOffice is an office suite built with Rust and the GPU. This playground runs it with WebAssembly and WebGPU: open the sample documents below, or your own files. Nothing is installed, and nothing is uploaded.

This browser does not support WebGPU. Please use the latest Chrome, Edge or Safari (26 or later).

About this playground

Browsers

Needs WebGPU: the latest Chrome or Edge, or Safari 26 or later.

First load

The app is downloaded the first time you open a document, so it may take a moment. After that it opens from your browser's cache.

Desktop app

Some features, such as PDF export and video playback, are available only in the desktop app.
