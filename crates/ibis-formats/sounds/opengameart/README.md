# Sounds from OpenGameArt (CC0)

Two sounds whose pages on OpenGameArt show the licence **CC0** (`LICENSE` is CC0 1.0 Universal's legal code). Downloaded on 2026-11-19.

| shipped file | made from | author, page |
|---|---|---|
| `typewriter.ogg` | `typewriter3.wav` | "Typewriter sounds" by Cassie-OrbitGames (2025-09-06, "recorded these on my phone for one of my games"), <https://opengameart.org/content/typewriter-sounds>, licence CC0 |
| `applause.ogg` | `Well Done CCBY3.ogg` | "Well Done" by qubodup (Iwan 'qubodup' Gabovitch), <https://opengameart.org/content/well-done>, licence CC0 |

**Well Done's licence.** The file was published in 2013 under CC-BY 3.0 (its name still says `CCBY3`). The author changed the page's licence to CC0, and the page says so: "Changed to CC0 on 2024-10-05. Feel free to
ignore the "CCBY3" in the filename now." The Ogg file carries no licence tag (only the artist, qubodup.net, and the year 2013). The page's licence line (CC0) and this statement of the author are the evidence for using it.

What was changed: made mono, 44.1 kHz, cut to at most 3 s, faded for 4 ms at the start and 30 ms at the end, normalised to a peak of -3 dBFS and encoded as Ogg Vorbis (`scripts/transition-sounds/build.py`).
